Application UDP payload: 128 bytes, including SeqTsHeader.
Whole-run goodput denominator: 30 seconds; decimal kbit/s.
Time-series bins: reception times in [start, end), width 1 second.
Delay includes received packets only; losses have no delay value.
Before/from 10 s groups use packet SEND time, not arrival time.
For stationary runs, 10 s is a matched analysis boundary only.
Each run sends 10 packets before 10 s and 20 from 10 s onward.
Group delay results average per-run means; valid-run counts are saved.
Min/max describe observed run ranges, not confidence intervals.
Monitoring counters cover neighbour probes, unicast validation,
destination confirmations, onward reports, and failure reports.
Monitoring payload is 64 bytes per socket-accepted message.
Socket acceptance does not prove transmission or delivery.
These counters EXCLUDE IP/UDP/MAC headers, AODV, ARP, ACKs and retries.
Largest observed arrival gap is not an exact route-repair time.
